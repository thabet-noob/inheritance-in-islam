Test Case #103
http://inheritance.ilmsummit.org/projects/inheritance/Results.aspx?TestCaseID=103

Heirs:
  - Daughter x1
  - FullSister x2
  - PaternalBrother x1

Shares by Category:
  - Daughter                     1/2      50%
  - FullSister                   1/2      50%
  - PaternalBrother              0/2      0%

Shares by Individual:
  - Daughter                     1/2      50%
  - FullSister 1                 1/4      25%
  - FullSister 2                 1/4      25%
  - PaternalBrother              0        0%

Calculation Steps:
  1. FullSister blocks PaternalBrother from receiving any share. (Rule 13)
  2. Daughter gets the prescribed share of 1/2 (Rule 3a).
  3. Distribute the remaining shares according to relative priority (Rule 14)
  4. FullSister is the only heir remaining receiving share of 1/2 (Rule 14).
