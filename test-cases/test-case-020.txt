Test Case #20
http://inheritance.ilmsummit.org/projects/inheritance/Results.aspx?TestCaseID=20

Heirs:
  - Wife x2
  - PaternalGrandMother x1
  - MaternalGrandMother x1
  - MaternalBrother x2

Shares by Category:
  - Wife                         1/4      25%
  - PaternalGrandMother          1/8      12.5%
  - MaternalGrandMother          1/8      12.5%
  - MaternalBrother              1/2      50%

Shares by Individual:
  - Wife 1                       1/8      12.5%
  - Wife 2                       1/8      12.5%
  - PaternalGrandMother          1/8      12.5%
  - MaternalGrandMother          1/8      12.5%
  - MaternalBrother 1            1/4      25%
  - MaternalBrother 2            1/4      25%

Calculation Steps:
  1. Wife gets the prescribed share of 1/4 (Rule 2a).
  2. Pateral Grandmother gets the prescribed share of 1/12 (Rule 8b).
  3. Maternal Grandmother gets the prescribed share of 1/12 (Rule 9b).
  4. Maternal Siblings gets the prescribed share of 1/3 (Rule 12b).
  5. Maternal Brothers gets 1/6 & Maternal Sisters gets 1/6 - the ratio 1:1 (Rule 33).
  6. Distribute the remaining shares according to relative priority (Rule 14)
  7. Total shares are still remaining to be distributed. Shares should now be increased proportionally among all heirs except the spouse (Rule 19).
