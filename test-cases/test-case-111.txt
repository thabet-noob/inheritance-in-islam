Heirs:
  - Husband x1
  - Mother x1
  - FullSister x1
  - PaternalSister x1
  - MaternalSister x2

Shares by Category:
  - Husband                      3/10     30%
  - Mother                       1/10     10%
  - FullSister                   3/10     30%
  - PaternalSister               1/10     10%
  - MaternalSister               1/5      20%

Shares by Individual:
  - Husband                      3/10     30%
  - Mother                       1/10     10%
  - FullSister                   3/10     30%
  - PaternalSister               1/10     10%
  - MaternalSister 1             1/10     10%
  - MaternalSister 2             1/10     10%

Calculation Steps:
  1. Husband gets the prescribed share of 1/2 (Rule 1a).
  2. Mother gets the prescribed share of 1/6 (Rule 6b).
  3. Full Sister gets the prescribed share of 1/2 (Rule 10a).
  4. Paternal Sister gets the prescribed share of 1/6 (Rule 11c).
  5. Maternal Siblings gets the prescribed share of 1/3 (Rule 12b).
  6. Maternal Brothers gets 1/6 & Maternal Sisters gets 1/6 - the ratio 1:1 (Rule 33).
  7. Total shares have exceeded 100%. Shares need to be reduced proportionally (Rule 18).
