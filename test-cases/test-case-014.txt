Test Case #14
http://inheritance.ilmsummit.org/projects/inheritance/Results.aspx?TestCaseID=14

Heirs:
  - PaternalGrandMother x1
  - FullSister x1
  - MaternalBrother x1

Shares by Category:
  - PaternalGrandMother          1/5      20%
  - FullSister                   3/5      60%
  - MaternalBrother              1/5      20%

Shares by Individual:
  - PaternalGrandMother          1/5      20%
  - FullSister                   3/5      60%
  - MaternalBrother              1/5      20%

Calculation Steps:
  1. Paternal Grandmother gets the prescribed share of 1/6 (Rule 8a).
  2. Full Sister gets the prescribed share of 1/2 (Rule 10a).
  3. Maternal Brother gets the prescribed share of 1/6 (Rule 12a).
  4. Distribute the remaining shares according to relative priority (Rule 14)
  5. Total shares are still remaining to be distributed. Shares should now be increased proportionally among all heirs except the spouse (Rule 19).
