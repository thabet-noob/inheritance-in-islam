  - Wife                         1/4      25%
  - FullBrother                  3/5      60%
  - FullSister                   3/20     15%
  - FullUncle                    0/20     0%

Shares by Individual:
  - Wife                         1/4      25%
  - FullBrother 1                3/10     30%
  - FullBrother 2                3/10     30%
  - FullSister                   3/20     15%
  - FullUncle                    0        0%

Calculation Steps:
  1. FullBrother blocks FullUncle from receiving any share. (Rule 13)
  2. Wife gets the prescribed share of 1/4 (Rule 2a).
  3. Distribute the remaining shares according to relative priority (Rule 14)
  4. Joint Ta'seeb of FullBrother and FullSister (Rule 44).
  5. FullBrother and FullSister share in the ratio of 2:1 (Rule 15)
  6. FullBrother gets the share of 3/5 and FullSister gets the share of 3/20. (Rule 15, 44)
