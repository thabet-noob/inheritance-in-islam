http://inheritance.ilmsummit.org/projects/inheritance/Results.aspx?TestCaseID=15

Heirs:
  - Daughter x1
  - GrandDaughter x2

Shares by Category:
  - Daughter                     3/4      75%
  - GrandDaughter                1/4      25%

Shares by Individual:
  - Daughter                     3/4      75%
  - GrandDaughter 1              1/8      12.5%
  - GrandDaughter 2              1/8      12.5%

Calculation Steps:
  1. Daughter gets the prescribed share of 1/2 (Rule 3a).
  2. Daughter gets the prescribed share of 1/6 (Rule 4c).
  3. Distribute the remaining shares according to relative priority (Rule 14)
  4. Total shares are still remaining to be distributed. Shares should now be increased proportionally among all heirs except the spouse (Rule 19).
