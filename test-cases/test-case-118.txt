http://inheritance.ilmsummit.org/projects/inheritance/Results.aspx?TestCaseID=118

Heirs:
  - Daughter x3

Shares by Category:
  - Daughter                     1/1      100%

Shares by Individual:
  - Daughter 1                   1/3      33.33%
  - Daughter 2                   1/3      33.33%
  - Daughter 3                   1/3      33.33%

Calculation Steps:
  1. Daughter gets the prescribed share of 2/3 (Rule 3b).
  2. Distribute the remaining shares according to relative priority (Rule 14)
  3. Total shares are still remaining to be distributed. Shares should now be increased proportionally among all heirs except the spouse (Rule 19).
