http://inheritance.ilmsummit.org/projects/inheritance/Results.aspx?TestCaseID=121

Heirs:
  - Wife x1
  - Daughter x2
  - Mother x1

Shares by Category:
  - Wife                         1/8      12.5%
  - Daughter                     7/10     70%
  - Mother                       7/40     17.5%

Shares by Individual:
  - Wife                         1/8      12.5%
  - Daughter 1                   7/20     35%
  - Daughter 2                   7/20     35%
  - Mother                       7/40     17.5%

Calculation Steps:
  1. Wife gets the prescribed share of 1/8 (Rule 2b).
  2. Daughter gets the prescribed share of 2/3 (Rule 3b).
  3. Mother gets the prescribed share of 1/6 (Rule 6b).
  4. Distribute the remaining shares according to relative priority (Rule 14)
  5. Total shares are still remaining to be distributed. Shares should now be increased proportionally among all heirs except the spouse (Rule 19).
