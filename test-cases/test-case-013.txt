Test Case #13
http://inheritance.ilmsummit.org/projects/inheritance/Results.aspx?TestCaseID=13

Heirs:
  - Mother x1
  - FullSister x1

Shares by Category:
  - Mother                       2/5      40%
  - FullSister                   3/5      60%

Shares by Individual:
  - Mother                       2/5      40%
  - FullSister                   3/5      60%

Calculation Steps:
  1. Mother gets the prescribed share of 1/3 (Rule 6a).
  2. Full Sister gets the prescribed share of 1/2 (Rule 10a).
  3. Distribute the remaining shares according to relative priority (Rule 14)
  4. Total shares are still remaining to be distributed. Shares should now be increased proportionally among all heirs except the spouse (Rule 19).
