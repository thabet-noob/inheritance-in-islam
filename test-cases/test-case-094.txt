Heirs:
  - Wife x1
  - Daughter x1
  - FullBrother x1
  - FullUncle x1

Shares by Category:
  - Wife                         1/8      12.5%
  - Daughter                     1/2      50%
  - FullBrother                  3/8      37.5%
  - FullUncle                    0/8      0%

Shares by Individual:
  - Wife                         1/8      12.5%
  - Daughter                     1/2      50%
  - FullBrother                  3/8      37.5%
  - FullUncle                    0        0%

Calculation Steps:
  1. FullBrother blocks FullUncle from receiving any share. (Rule 13)
  2. Wife gets the prescribed share of 1/8 (Rule 2b).
  3. Daughter gets the prescribed share of 1/2 (Rule 3a).
  4. Distribute the remaining shares according to relative priority (Rule 14)
  5. FullBrother is the only heir remaining receiving share of 3/8 (Rule 14).
